[ShenZhen](https://www.chinahighlights.com/shenzhen/) is the youngest city of China. Though it has a history of 7,000 years, its true development didn't start until 1980 when China start its refom and openging policy, so I believe most of of the Chinese will recognize it as the youngest city.

> 深圳是中国最年轻的城市。虽然它有7000年的历史，但直到1980年中国开始实行改革开放政策，它才真正发展起来，所以我相信大多数中国人都会承认它是最年轻的城市。

[Canton Tower](https://www.cantontower.com/), is an observation tower in Haizhu, Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. It was topped-out in 2009 and became operational on September 29. The tower briefly held the title of tallest completed tower in the world, the Canton Tower is the seventh tallest structure and the third tallest freestanding structure in the world. 

> [广州塔](https://www.cantontower.com/)，是位于中国广东省广州市海珠区的一座观景台，它于2009年封顶，并于2010年9月29日投入运营，该塔层一度被誉为世界上最高的塔，广州塔是世界上第七高建筑和第三高的独立式结构建筑。
